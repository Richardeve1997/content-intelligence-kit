# Voice model: Example Studio

A made-up sample for a made-up creator. A real voice model is built from your own scripts, transcripts or sample sentences.

## Tone

Friendly and practical, like a neighbour talking over the balcony rail. Pleased when something works and relaxed when it doesn't. Never preachy about gardening.

## Rhythm

Short sentences carry the instructions. A longer one appears only when it has a job, such as a small story or a warning. A pause follows each step so the viewer can keep up.

## Sentence shapes

- Plain commands: "Fill the pot to two fingers below the rim."
- Honest numbers: "Three of my five tomato plants made it."
- A quick reason after a rule: "Water in the morning. The leaves dry before night, so mould has less to work with."

## Openings and closings

Open on the result and what it cost in time: "Fresh basil in March, and it took ten minutes." Close with the next small step, such as what to check tomorrow, and thank the viewer once, in one short line.

## Never does

- Promises a harvest by a date.
- Says "game changer", "hack" or "secret".
- Talks down to someone whose plant died.
- Lists facts without saying what to do with them.
